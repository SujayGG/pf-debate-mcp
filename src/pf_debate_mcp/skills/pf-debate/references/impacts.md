# Terminal impacts: standard chains, weighing, answers

Use this to build impact-first cases ("centered around a nuclear impact") and to answer them. The chains below are *shapes*. Every link still needs a real card from `search_cards` or `fetch_source` + `cut_card`. Good library queries are listed for each impact; add `sort="popular"` to get the cards most teams trust.

## Nuclear war
**Wins on:** magnitude, reversibility, short-circuit (it ends other impacts).
**Loses on:** probability (long chains), empirics (deterrence has held since 1945).

Common scenarios, each as chain → library queries:
- **US–China / Taiwan:** a US capability or credibility shift → China perceives a window or US weakness → Taiwan or South China Sea conflict → escalation to nuclear use. Queries: "Taiwan conflict escalation nuclear", "US China war goes nuclear", "China window of opportunity".
- **Tech/AI race → China:** US loses its AI or compute lead → China gains military AI advantage → deterrence destabilized → war. Queries: "AI lead China military advantage", "compute race China", "AI arms race escalation".
- **Russia–NATO:** instability or credibility → Baltic/Ukraine escalation → nuclear use. Queries: "Russia NATO escalation nuclear", "tactical nuclear weapons use Russia".
- **India–Pakistan:** crisis (terror attack, water, Kashmir) → conventional war → nuclear exchange (the South Asia nuclear winter studies). Queries: "India Pakistan nuclear war nuclear winter".
- **Proliferation (prolif):** allies lose US security guarantees, or energy/tech spreads → new nuclear states → more flashpoints and accidents. Queries: "proliferation cascade", "prolif causes nuclear war".
- **Econ collapse → war:** recession → diversionary war / great-power tension (Harris & Burrows 09 is the classic, very widely read). Queries: "economic decline causes war", "diversionary war".
- **Terminal cards:** "nuclear winter extinction", "limited nuclear war escalation", "nuclear war kills billions" (Robock/Toon-style studies).

**Answers (neg to a nuke-war impact):** Deterrence and MAD hold (empirics). Economic interdependence checks war. Chains multiply probabilities (four 50% links is about 6%). Leaders are rational and de-escalate (crisis management, hotlines). Nuclear winter is contested. Their scenario is non-unique (tensions exist regardless).

## Extinction / existential risk
Sources: nuclear winter, AI misalignment, engineered pandemics, runaway climate.
**Wins on:** magnitude, "extinction first" framing (it forecloses all future value).
**Loses on:** probability; the judge's intuition (lay judges find it silly).
Queries: "extinction outweighs", "existential risk prioritize", "AI existential risk", "bioweapons extinction".
**Answers:** Probability first (tiny risks multiplied by infinity breaks decision-making, the Pascal's mugging argument). Resilience literature says humans survive even extreme scenarios.

## Climate change
Chains: emissions ↑ → warming → food, water and migration crises → conflict and deaths. For data centers: power demand ↑ → fossil generation kept online → emissions; also water use in drought regions.
Queries: "data center emissions fossil", "climate change conflict", "warming tipping points", "climate deaths".
**Answers:** Clean energy additions (PPAs, nuclear deals) offset demand. The emissions are small relative to the global total, so it's non-unique. Tech itself enables mitigation (AI for grids and materials).

## Economy
Chains: GDP/jobs/investment ↓ → recession → poverty, deaths (unemployment is linked to mortality) and war (see diversionary war above).
Queries: "recession deaths unemployment mortality", "economic decline war", "AI investment GDP growth", "construction jobs data center".
**Answers:** Resilient economy; effects are marginal; investment relocates rather than disappears; the benefits flow to shareholders, not workers (distributional turn).

## Poverty / structural violence (SV)
Framing: structural violence (poverty, racism) kills more people, invisibly and every day, than wars do. Prefer the probable and certain.
Chains: energy prices ↑ → energy poverty → deaths; local harms to marginalized communities (siting, pollution, water).
Queries: "structural violence outweighs", "energy poverty deaths", "utility bills rise data centers", "environmental racism siting".
**Answers:** Util-frame; war causes structural violence; our impact is also systemic.

## Disease / pandemics
Chains: research or health-system capacity ↓, or climate/habitat ↑ → pandemic risk.
Queries: "pandemic extinction", "AI drug discovery", "zoonotic spillover".

## AI risk (topic-relevant)
Two directions. (a) Racing without safety → misaligned AI → catastrophe, so slowing is good (aff on a moratorium). (b) If the US slows, China leads → worse AI governance and military imbalance (neg).
Queries: "AI race safety", "pause AI development", "China AI governance", "compute governance".

## Democracy / rights
Chains: surveillance or tech power concentration → democratic backsliding → war (democratic peace theory).
Queries: "democracy decline war", "democratic peace", "tech monopoly power democracy".

## Weighing cheat sheet
| Your impact | vs. | Best weighing |
|---|---|---|
| Nuke war | Structural violence | Magnitude + reversibility; war causes SV (turns case) |
| Structural violence | Nuke war | Probability + timeframe (SV is happening now); SV framework |
| Econ | Climate | Timeframe (recession is immediate); econ growth funds climate adaptation (pre-req) |
| Climate | Econ | Irreversibility; climate damages the economy (turns case) |
| Extinction | Anything | Extinction-first framing, plus a probability defense of your own chain |
