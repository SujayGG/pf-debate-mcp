Use it inside your own AI

Paste this connector link into your AI app's connector settings to give it the same tools.

  • Claude: Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector. Or install pf-debate.mcpb in Claude Desktop.
  • ChatGPT: Settings, then Apps & Connectors, then Advanced, then turn on Developer mode and add the link.

Claude requires users to be 18+ (free plans allow 1 custom connector). ChatGPT connectors need a paid plan with Developer mode and may not work on teen accounts. The web app has no such limits.
